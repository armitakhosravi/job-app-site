Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Job App is a personal recruitment assistant used to discover job opportunities and prepare application documents for human review.

Google Data Access

With the account holder's authorization, Job App uses read-only Gmail access to identify relevant job alerts and application-selection replies.

How Information Is Used

Relevant email information is used to discover job opportunities, identify selected positions, and coordinate application preparation.

Data Storage and Security

Job App stores authorization credentials and relevant operational records in its private execution environment. Access is restricted to authorized operators.

Third-Party Services

Job App uses external services for job research, document generation, and email delivery. These may include OpenAI, search providers, and Resend. Relevant professional candidate information may be shared with these providers when necessary to prepare application documents.

Gmail message data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for unrelated purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Operational records are retained as needed to support application preparation, auditing, and duplicate prevention. The account holder can revoke Gmail authorization through Google Account settings and arrange deletion of locally stored information.
